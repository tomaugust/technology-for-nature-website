Maya designs and tests practical technology for environmental fieldwork. She works with researchers, land managers, and technical partners to adapt sensors, cameras, and deployment workflows for real monitoring needs.

Her current focus is making biodiversity monitoring systems easier to deploy, document, maintain, and reuse across projects.
