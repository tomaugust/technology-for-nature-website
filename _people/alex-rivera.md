Alex works on turning sensor and survey data into clear, reusable evidence for environmental decision making. Their work focuses on robust data workflows, quality checks, and dashboards that help researchers and partners understand monitoring results.

Recent work includes prototype analytics for automated biodiversity monitoring and reusable data structures for long-term field sensor deployments.
